Italo Jewelry Combiner

Italo Jewelry Combiner is a private desktop application developed by LuckyCat for preparing jewelry product content and managing Pins on the account owner's Pinterest account.

What the application does

  • Processes product image galleries and creates Pinterest-ready image collages.
  • Prepares Pin titles, descriptions, and destination links.
  • Connects to Pinterest using the account owner's authorization.
  • Reads the account owner's boards and creates Pins only after the account owner manually confirms each publication.

Access and availability

This application is an internal tool. It is not offered as a public service and is not intended for use by third parties.

For information about data handling, see our Privacy Policy.
